Comuginator Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their family account and associated data directly inside the Comuginator application.

How to delete data

  1. Open the Family screen
  2. Open the menu
  3. Select "Delete family"

Deleting a family removes:

  • family members
  • devices
  • AAC messages
  • AAC replies
  • uploaded images
  • child home configuration

Some technical logs and backups may be retained temporarily for operational and security purposes.
