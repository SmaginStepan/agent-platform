Privacy Policy for Comuginator

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Comuginator is a family communication application that helps users communicate using AAC cards, images, and visual messages.

Information We Collect

The app may collect and process:

  • Family account information
  • User names and roles inside a family group
  • Device identifiers and device status information
  • Push notification tokens
  • AAC messages and replies
  • Uploaded images used as communication cards

Camera and Media Access

Comuginator may request access to the camera and media storage so users can create and upload images for AAC cards and family communication.

These permissions are used only for the app's communication features.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used to:

  • Provide family communication features
  • Synchronize AAC cards, messages, and replies
  • Deliver push notifications
  • Maintain device connection and app functionality

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Data Sharing

Data is shared only with members of the same family group inside the app and with technical service providers necessary to operate the app infrastructure.

Children and Families

Comuginator is intended for family communication and may be used by children with the involvement of parents or caregivers.

Data Deletion

Users can delete their family account and associated data inside the app. Users may also request deletion by contacting us.

Account and data deletion information is available here: Account and Data Deletion

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect user data. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections where applicable.

Changes
